# 17. Legal and policy drafts

> These are implementation outlines, not legal advice. A qualified Nigerian lawyer should review and adapt them before public use.

## Purpose

Provide the minimum policy structure needed for a trustworthy student-facing launch.

## Terms of Use outline

1. Service description and account responsibilities.
2. Student ownership and accuracy responsibility.
3. AI assistance limitations and provider dependencies.
4. Academic integrity: no fake SIWES, signatures or unsupported claims.
5. Evidence/company confidentiality obligations.
6. Acceptable use, abuse limits and suspension.
7. Exports, storage, deletion and service availability.
8. Intellectual property and feedback.
9. Liability, governing law and dispute route.
10. Changes and contact.

## Privacy Policy outline

Use the structure in [08-security-and-privacy.md](./08-security-and-privacy.md): data categories, purposes, legal basis, processors/transfer, LLM disclosure, Telegram/OAuth, storage/retention, rights, security, children/age policy if applicable, changes and contact.

## AI-use disclosure draft

“SIWES Companion uses automated systems to organize text you provide. The system may ask for clarification or produce an incorrect draft. Review every draft before saving or exporting. It must not invent an activity, tool, responsibility, achievement, project result or signature. Your original note remains stored as the source record. Do not upload confidential material you are not permitted to share.”

## Academic integrity statement draft

“The student is responsible for the truthfulness of submitted SIWES records. This product assists with organization and communication; it does not verify attendance or replace supervisor review. Do not use it to fabricate activities, signatures, evidence or results.”

## Open questions

- Minimum user age and consent language for the intended institutions.
- Official support/contact identity and response times.
- Legal basis and processor agreements for cross-border LLM/storage processing.
